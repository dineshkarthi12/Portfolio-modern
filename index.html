About

I care about the half-second between a scroll and a response.

I'm a front-end engineer working on the interaction layer — the part of a product people actually feel. Six years across design systems, WebGL-adjacent canvas work, and the unglamorous performance passes that make the rest of it possible.

Most of my work sits between design and engineering: turning a static comp into something that has weight and rhythm, without shipping a 4 MB bundle to do it. The hero above is a good example — 150 JPEGs, a canvas, and a lerp.

  • 6+ years shipping front-end
  • 40+ production interfaces
  • ~98 median Lighthouse perf
